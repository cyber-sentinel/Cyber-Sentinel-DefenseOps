# Cyber-Sentinel Brand Architecture

## Cyber-Sentinel

The umbrella identity for Ali RahimDabagh's open cyber defense engineering and intelligence projects.

## Cyber-Sentinel-DefenseOps

**Purpose:** Open cyber defense operations engineering.

Primary responsibilities:

- detection engineering;
- threat hunting;
- rule/query portability;
- validation and test fixtures;
- DFIR content;
- incident-response content;
- cyber deception;
- defensive automation;
- reproducible labs.

DefenseOps is the engineering source of truth for defensive artifacts.

## Cyber-Sentinel-Atlas

**Purpose:** Planned international cyber defense intelligence and analyst platform.

Atlas will provide:

- global cyber defense search;
- Windows/Sysmon/Linux/cloud/container knowledge;
- event and artifact reference;
- MITRE ATT&CK / D3FEND / CAR relationships;
- detection and hunt discovery;
- DFIR investigation guidance;
- offline-first access;
- responsive web/PWA/desktop experiences;
- API and CLI interfaces;
- source-cited AI-assisted navigation.

## Product Principle

Do not duplicate knowledge between DefenseOps and Atlas.

DefenseOps should expose structured, versioned, validated content. Atlas should index, relate, search, visualize, and operationalize that content.
